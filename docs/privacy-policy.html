Privacy Policy — Pookie POS

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Pookie POS ("the app") is a point-of-sale application for small businesses, developed by Hadinata Jenta ("the developer"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Summary

The app works completely offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your own device.

Information the app stores on your device

To run a point of sale, the app stores the business data you enter locally, in a private database on the device:

  • Product data: names, categories, units, prices, cost prices, stock levels, and optional product photos
  • Sales data: transactions, items, totals, discounts, tax, payment method, and returns
  • Business records: customer names, debts, and debt payments
  • Store settings and preferences

This data is never uploaded anywhere. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data over the network. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this data permanently, so keep your own backups.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account, email address, phone number, or password
  • No location data
  • No access to contacts beyond the single contact you choose to import into a customer form
  • No advertising identifiers and no analytics or crash reporting
  • No usage tracking of any kind

Permissions and why they are requested

  • BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN — used to discover, connect to, and print to a Bluetooth thermal receipt printer. No Bluetooth data is stored or transmitted.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only to show an offline notice inside the app. It reads the connection state; it does not send anything.
  • READ_CONTACTS — requested only when you tap Pilih dari kontak while adding a customer. The app reads the name and phone number of the single contact you pick, to fill the form. Contacts are never uploaded, scanned in bulk, or shared.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and older only) — used to save a report export file when you choose to export a report. On Android 10 and newer, exports use the system download storage and no permission is required.

Files you export

When you export a sales report as CSV or Excel, or an app backup as JSON, the app writes the file to the location you choose in the system file picker, typically the Download folder. The file contains your business data and is under your control. Sharing that file with anyone else is your decision.

Third parties

The current version contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party data collection SDKs. If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play Data Safety section will reflect it.

Children

The app is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you are in full control of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android settings, deletes all app data from the device.

Changes to this policy

Any future change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
